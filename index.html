About

Ten years of asking "but why does it do that?"

I've been writing about Android since the days when "flagship" meant a removable battery. At Android Headlines I review the devices people actually buy, chase down the news that matters, and try to explain the platform in a way that doesn't require a computer science degree.

When I'm not testing a phone, I'm usually testing another phone. Or a watch. Or a pair of earbuds I'll lose within the month.
